# Visriva Marketing Master Workflow

**Your operating system:** You give one voice note or message → AI creates content → you approve → tools post & reply → you close on WhatsApp.

---

## The stack (all free or low-cost to start)

| Tool | Role | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| ChatGPT / Claude | Content brain (scripts, captions, DMs) | Free–₹2k/mo |
| Canva | Visuals | Free–₹500/mo |
| Meta Business Suite | Schedule Feed + Stories | Free |
| ManyChat | Auto-reply when people DM you | Free tier |
| Google Sheets | Lead tracker (`lead-tracker.csv`) | Free |
| Meta Ads Manager | Paid reach to `/reserve` | ₹500+/day |
| Your website | Book + owner WhatsApp alert | Already built |

**You do NOT need:** Instagram login bots, mass cold-DM software, or follow/unfollow farms.

## Daily checklist (25 minutes)

### Morning (10 min)
- [ ] Post 1–3 Stories (from calendar or improvise BTS)
- [ ] Reply to any DMs ManyChat didn't fully qualify
- [ ] Check Meta Ads — pause if CPC > ₹25

### Afternoon (15 min)
- [ ] Find 10 planner/venue accounts
- [ ] Comment genuinely on 10 posts
- [ ] Send 10 personalized DMs
- [ ] Update `lead-tracker.csv`

### Evening (optional 5 min)
- [ ] Engage with comments on your Reel
- [ ] Repost guest story to your Stories (with tag)

## "One command" examples (say this to ChatGPT)

**Weekly batch:**
> "It's week 2 of August. Create my Visriva marketing pack — 4 Reels about wedding magnets, corporate gifting, setup BTS, and FAQ. Include Stories, planner DMs for Bangalore luxury planners, and Meta ad copy. CTA: DM EVENT and visriva.com/reserve."

**Single Reel urgent:**
> "Write a 20-second Reel script for Visriva about sangeet night energy. Hook in 3 seconds, caption, hashtags, Canva on-screen text."

**Outreach day:**
> "Give me 10 Instagram search terms for wedding planners in Bengaluru and a unique DM opener for each account type."

**Ad refresh:**
> "My wedding ad CTR is 0.4%. Write 3 new primary texts and 2 headlines for Meta ads targeting Bengaluru brides 25–35."

## ManyChat setup (one-time, 45 min)

1. Sign up at manychat.com → Connect Instagram Business
2. Copy flows from `dm-automation-flows.md`:
   - Default welcome
   - Keywords: EVENT, PLANNER, PRICE, DATE, CORPORATE
3. Set Instagram bio: `DM "EVENT" for packages`
4. Test from your personal account
5. Enable email notification for new leads

---

## Meta Ads setup (one-time, 30 min)

Follow `meta-ads-checklist.md` step by step.

**Minimum viable test:** ₹500/day × 7 days → Wedding traffic → `/reserve`
