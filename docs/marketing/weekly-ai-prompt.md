# Weekly AI Content Prompt

Copy everything below into ChatGPT or Claude every **Sunday**. Replace `[WEEK OF]` and add any real event details from the week.

---

```
You are the marketing strategist for Visriva Live Station — luxury live experiential gifting & photo booths in Bengaluru + pan-India.

BRAND
- Instagram: @visriva.co
- Book: https://www.visriva.com/reserve
- WhatsApp: +91 88844 84828
- Services: Instant Photo Booth, Live Magnet Station, Keychain Station, Mug Printing, Tote/T-Shirt Station
- Audience: wedding couples (25–35), corporate HR/events, wedding planners & decorators in Bengaluru
- Tone: premium, cinematic, warm — never cheesy, never "cheap deals"
- Colors: emerald #011F15, gold #D4AF37

WEEK: [WEEK OF e.g. 11 Aug 2026]

Create this week's marketing pack:

## 1. REELS (4 scripts)
For each: Hook (first 3 sec on-screen text), shot list (what to film), voiceover/caption script (max 30 sec), caption for IG, 15 hashtags (mix broad + Bengaluru), best posting day/time IST.

Topics to rotate: event BTS, guest reactions, planner tip, myth-busting ("photo booths are outdated"), corporate gifting, magnet/keychain ASMR, setup timelapse, "what ₹X actually includes".

## 2. STORIES (7 days)
For each day: 3 story frames with exact on-screen text, sticker suggestion (poll/quiz/slider), CTA (link sticker to /reserve or "DM EVENT").

## 3. CAROUSEL (1 post)
5-slide educational carousel: "5 reasons luxury weddings add a live gifting station" — slide headlines + body copy.

## 4. DM AUTO-REPLIES
Update keyword replies for: EVENT, PLANNER, PRICE, DATE, CORPORATE — each under 300 characters, end with clear next step.

## 5. PLANNER OUTREACH
10 Instagram account *types* to search in Bengaluru this week + 1 personalized DM opener each (mention engaging with their content first).

## 6. WHATSAPP FOLLOW-UP
3 messages for: (a) warm lead who asked price, (b) lead who went silent 48h, (c) post-consultation thank you + contract link.

## 7. META AD COPY
2 ad variants (wedding + corporate): primary text, headline, description, CTA button — optimized for traffic to /reserve.

Keep all CTAs driving to DM keyword EVENT or /reserve. No fake testimonials. No competitor bashing.
```
