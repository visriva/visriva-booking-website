## Canva workflow

1. Create Brand Kit: colors `#011F15` + `#D4AF37`, upload logo
2. Duplicate one Reel template per week
3. Export MP4 for Reels, PNG for carousels
4. Schedule in Meta Business Suite

**Canva search terms:** "luxury instagram reel", "wedding reel template", "minimal gold black story"
