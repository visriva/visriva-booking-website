# Planner & Vendor Outreach — Bengaluru

**Goal:** 10 personalized DMs per weekday = ~200 conversations/month.

**Rule:** Engage first (like/comment one real post), then DM. Never copy-paste without their name or a specific detail.

---

## Who to target (search on Instagram)

### Tier A — Wedding planners (highest value)
Search: `wedding planner bangalore`, `luxury wedding planner bengaluru`, `wedding planner karnataka`

| Type | What to look for | Why |
|------|------------------|-----|
| Luxury wedding planners | 10k–100k followers, hotel/destination weddings | High budgets, repeat bookings |
| Boutique planners | 2k–20k, strong Reels, South Indian weddings | Local Bengaluru events |
| Sangeet specialists | Dance/sangeet focused planners | Need engagement stations |

### Tier B — Decor & production
Search: `wedding decorator bangalore`, `event decor bengaluru`, `mandap designer`

- Floral decorators
- Stage/backdrop companies
- Wedding production houses

*They recommend vendors to clients — one good relationship = many events.*

### Tier C — Venues & hospitality
Search: `wedding venue bangalore`, `banquet hall bangalore`, `resort wedding karnataka`

- Boutique hotels, farmhouses, convention centres
- DM their **events/sales** account, not generic hotel page

### Tier D — Corporate event agencies
Search: `corporate events bangalore`, `brand activation india`, `MICE bangalore`

- HR event vendors
- Agency studios doing activations

### Tier E — Adjacent vendors (referral swap)
- Photographers & cinematographers (non-competing — you complement them)
- DJs & entertainment
- Cake/dessert tables
- Invitation designers

**Offer:** "We refer clients who ask for vendors — happy to mention you when decor/photo comes up."

---

## How to build your list (30 min once)

1. Open Instagram → Search each term above.
2. Save accounts to a Collection named **Visriva Outreach**.
3. Add rows to `lead-tracker.csv` (name, handle, tier, date contacted).
4. Prioritize accounts that posted in last 14 days (active).

---

## Daily routine (30 minutes)

| Step | Time | Action |
|------|------|--------|
| 1 | 5 min | Open Saved collection, pick 10 accounts |
| 2 | 10 min | Like + leave 1 genuine comment on recent post |
| 3 | 15 min | Send personalized DM (templates below) |
| 4 | — | Log in tracker; set follow-up date +3 days |

**Weekly:** Reply to everyone who responded before adding new cold outreach.

## DM templates

### Template 1 — Wedding planner (first touch)

```
Hi [Name], I came across your work on [specific event/post] — the [detail: mandap / colour palette / sangeet energy] was stunning.

I'm [Your name] from Visriva — we run luxury live gifting stations (photo booth, live magnets, keychains) for weddings in Bengaluru and pan-India.

If you ever need a premium guest-experience add-on for clients, I'd love to share our planner partner deck. No pressure at all.

Would it be okay if I sent a 1-page overview?
```

### Template 2 — Decorator / vendor

```
Hi [Name], love what you're building with [Agency] — especially [recent post detail].

We work alongside decor teams quite often — Visriva handles live print & gifting stations so guests leave with something physical (magnets, strips, keychains) that matches the event branding.

Open to being on your vendor list for Bengaluru events? Happy to send rates + setup specs.
```

## Comment examples (before DM)

Use these on their posts — not generic "Nice!":

- "The gold accent on the mandap paired with warm uplighting — very cohesive."
- "Sangeet energy in this Reel is infectious — guests must have loved it."
- "Love how you balanced traditional elements with a modern stage layout."

When relevant, name areas you serve: Indiranagar, Whitefield, Sarjapur, Electronic City, Yelahanka, Mysore Road, Nandi Hills destinations, resort weddings outside city.

## Referral incentive (optional — discuss before offering)

- Planner books 3 events → complimentary magnet upgrade on 4th
- Or flat referral fee per confirmed booking (agree offline, not in public posts)

## Red flags — skip these accounts

- No posts in 6+ months
- Only reposts, no original work
- Comments disabled / no engagement
- Clearly out of your market (budget decor under ₹50k total event — unlikely fit for Visriva luxury positioning)
